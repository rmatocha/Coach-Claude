Cadence governor
full power above rpm
Below that cadence, target power is reduced in proportion (never under 50%) so resistance can't snowball. It only acts when the target is above 80% of FTP.
Simulation mode
top of the band at rpm
On freeride and Simulation-mode intervals the grade is set so the top of the power band comes at this cadence. Ride a little under it to sit in the band, spin faster for more.
Audio cues
A bell with 10 s left in an interval, a tick each second for the last 5 s, and a higher tick when the next interval starts. Needs sound on and the ringer switch up.
Distance model
Average speed at 150 w mph
Distance is estimated from power with a physics model (air drag + rolling resistance + momentum), scaled so 150 w holds this speed on flat ground. It goes into the summary and the .fit file.
