Cadence governor
full power above rpm
Below that cadence, target power is reduced in proportion (never under 50%) so resistance can't snowball. It only acts when the target is above 80% of FTP.
Distance model
Average speed at 150 w mph
Distance is estimated from power with a physics model (air drag + rolling resistance + momentum), scaled so 150 w holds this speed on flat ground. It goes into the summary and the .fit file.
